The bay routine

Five stages. Same order, every car.

The order matters — skipping a stage is how paint gets scratched. This is the sequence we follow from the moment you park.

01

Walk-around

We look over the car with you, note existing marks, and agree on what the job includes before anything is touched.

02

Pre-rinse & foam

Loose grit is rinsed off and the car is covered in snow foam so dirt lifts away instead of being dragged across the paint.

03

Contact wash

Two buckets, clean mitts, top-down. Wheels and arches get their own tools so brake dust never reaches the bodywork.

04

Interior work

Vacuum, brush, treat and wipe. Fabric, leather and plastics each get the product they're meant to have.

05

Light check & handover

The car is dried with fresh microfibre and inspected under light. If we can see it, we fix it before you do.

Questions we get asked.

Where exactly are you?
480, near Golden Green Layout, Bengaluru 560061. If your maps app takes you somewhere odd, call 80887 35504 and we'll talk you in.
How long will it take?
An express wash — outside and inside — is usually under an hour. A full interior detail takes three to four hours because every panel, vent and seam is worked by hand. Paint work runs one to two days. We confirm your slot on WhatsApp before you drive in.
Is this safe for my paint?
Yes. pH-balanced foam, two-bucket washing, soft microfibre and controlled pressure. No spinning brushes and no reused dirty water — those are what put swirl marks into clear coat.
Which vehicles do you take?
Hatchbacks, sedans, SUVs and bikes. Size affects both time and price, so send your model on WhatsApp and we'll give you an exact number.
Do you need a booking?
Not strictly — walk in and if a bay is free, we'll start. Booking just means you don't wait. Weekends fill up fastest.
How do I pay?
Cash, UPI or card at the studio, after the work is done and you've seen it. Nothing is collected on this website.
